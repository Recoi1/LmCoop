**LmCoop 1.2.3: the mouse now aims the flashlight and vacuum up and down.**

- **Mouse up/down aims the flashlight and the vacuum**, at once, 1:1. Mouse up aims up, mouse down aims
  down. The beam and Luigi's arm follow, the aim stays where you leave it, and the camera does not
  move. Before (1.1.12 to 1.2.2) the mouse only moved the camera and never actually aimed the beam.
- The game's aim runs from slightly down (about 13 degrees) to almost straight up (about 70 degrees),
  roughly 550 pixels of mouse from level to straight up at the default sensitivity.
- The arrow keys (C-stick) and a gamepad's right stick still aim as the game does, and take over from
  the mouse while pressed; the mouse picks up from wherever the game leaves the aim.
- The idle animation stays off while the mouse is holding an aim.

Includes everything in 1.2.2 and earlier (W A S D relative to the mouse facing, instant mouse turning,
joining the host directly behind the loading screen). No retail game data is included; supply your own
USA disc dump (GLME01 revision 0).
