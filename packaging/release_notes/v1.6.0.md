**LmCoop 1.6.0: a mod browser, texture packs, and mods you can install from inside the game.**

- **Mods on the start menu** lists the Luigi's Mansion mods on GameBanana, each marked **Verified**, **Partial**,
  **Broken** or **Untested** by what the LmCoop developers have tested (a small list in the repository that can
  change between releases; see `docs/MODS.md`). Nothing is hosted by LmCoop: downloads come from GameBanana, on your
  PC, when you press Install.
- **Texture packs work.** Dolphin-style packs (the `tex1_..._hash_fmt.png` kind most GameBanana texture mods are)
  install from the Mods screen and switch on and off at once. Tested with Luigi Mansion Yocow Repainted 2024
  (2,355 textures): the foyer, Luigi and the HUD are repainted.
- **Loose-file mods work too** (an archive with a `files/` folder of replacement game files): the game serves them in
  place of the disc's while they are enabled. Tested with Luigi's Mansion Repainted: Console Edition (288 files).
- Installing downloads the `.zip`, `.7z` or `.rar` (only from gamebanana.com), unpacks it with Windows' own `tar`,
  checks that it holds one of those two kinds (otherwise it deletes it and says why), and turns it on. Installed
  packs are listed on the same screen with Enable / Disable / Remove.
- **What can not be installed:** mods that change the game's code (full overhauls such as Premium Deluxe, the
  randomizer, Beta Restoration) are patches for a whole disc and stay "Open page". Character packs (a mod's playable
  Luigi and voice) are still made with `tools/make_character_pack.py` (`docs/CHARACTERS.md`).
- Everything in 1.5.0 (character select, Steam play, the lobby) is included. Not tested: two Steam accounts together,
  two separate home networks, every room of the installed mods.

Includes everything in 1.5.0 and earlier. No retail game data is included; supply your own USA disc dump
(GLME01 revision 0).

Based on rob646464/luigis-mansion-recompiled, with Aurora by encounter and the
SomeJakeGuy/lm-decomp reference. libdatachannel (MPL-2.0), MbedTLS and nlohmann/json are used, and the
Steamworks SDK's `steam_api64.dll` is redistributed with the program; see NOTICE.md.
