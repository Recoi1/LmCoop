**LmCoop 1.5.0: choose your character, with character packs (e.g. a Luigi mod with his own voice).**

- **Character select** in the lobby: Singleplayer now opens a small lobby with a character row and Start, and the
  multiplayer lobby has the same row. It appears when a character pack is installed.
- **Character packs** replace the playable Luigi's model and voice with a mod's. Nothing of any mod is included:
  you make a pack from your own disc and the mod's `.xdelta` patch with `tools/make_character_pack.py` (Python 3;
  see `docs/CHARACTERS.md`). It was tested with Wega's Freaky Mansion v1.1: the playable Luigi and his voice banks
  load.
- **Limits:** cutscenes still show the original Luigi. In multiplayer the choice changes how the playable Luigi
  (the host's player) looks and sounds **on your own PC**; it is not sent to the other player, and the second
  player is still Mario. **Three-player multiplayer is not in this release.**
- Everything in 1.4.1 (Steam play, the lobby screen) is included. Not tested: two Steam accounts together, two
  separate home networks.

Includes everything in 1.4.1 and earlier. No retail game data is included; supply your own USA disc dump
(GLME01 revision 0).

Based on rob646464/luigis-mansion-recompiled, with Aurora by encounter and the
SomeJakeGuy/lm-decomp reference. libdatachannel (MPL-2.0), MbedTLS and nlohmann/json are used, and the
Steamworks SDK's `steam_api64.dll` is redistributed with the program; see NOTICE.md.
