**LmCoop 1.1.12 makes mouse up/down instant too, and fixes its direction.**

- **Instant vertical aim:** moving the mouse up/down now tilts the camera (and the flashlight and
  Poltergust aim, which follow it) at once, 1:1. 1.1.11 only fixed left/right; up/down still went
  through the game's slow, easing C-stick.
- **Mouse up now looks up.** Up/down were the wrong way round for a mouse: the game's stick pitches
  the camera down when pushed up, and the mouse copied that.
- The game limits the pitch to about 0 to 29 degrees, so the whole range is a short flick. The pitch
  starts from the game's own again whenever you enter a room.
- While you walk, the game still turns Luigi toward the way he is walking, which pulls against the
  mouse. Turning while standing or aiming is instant.

Includes everything in 1.1.11 and 1.1.10: instant left/right turning, no idle animation while you
use the mouse, the visual controller settings page, the start-screen version and update controls and
the Mario skinning fix. No retail game data is included; supply your own USA disc dump (GLME01
revision 0).
