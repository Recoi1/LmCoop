**LmCoop 1.7.4: the guest now follows the host out of E. Gadd's lab.**

- **Fixed a freeze when leaving the lab.** When the host picked a destination in "Where will you go, Luigi?" (the
  Mansion, Training Room or Gallery), the guest received the new map but never loaded it, because it still thought
  the lab cutscene was running. The host then re-sent the map forever (566 times in a 5-minute test) and the guest
  stayed behind in the lab. The guest now drops the old cutscene as soon as the host's map is a newer one and loads it.
- Checked with a host and a guest on one PC from a saved game: the guest follows into the mansion (worlds 2 and 3
  load), and both players' sticks move their own character on both PCs.
- Tracing for bug reports is no longer cut off after about 100 lines (`LM_ONLINE_TRACE=1`, `LM_POS_TRACE=1`).
- **Still open:** a second player's controller that does nothing (the network path works with scripted input, so it
  is probably controller detection on that PC: please send `last_run.log`), and a door that opens at the start.

Includes everything in 1.7.3 and earlier. No retail game data is included; supply your own USA disc dump
(GLME01 revision 0).

Based on rob646464/luigis-mansion-recompiled, with Aurora by encounter and the
SomeJakeGuy/lm-decomp reference. libdatachannel (MPL-2.0), MbedTLS and nlohmann/json are used, and the
Steamworks SDK's `steam_api64.dll` is redistributed with the program; see NOTICE.md.
