**LmCoop 1.1.7 fixes Mario's stretched or broken model in multiplayer.**

Mario's model is now selected before skinning matrices are calculated. Previously, a late
model swap could draw Mario using Luigi's skinning weights, distorting his body and clothing.

Download the Windows ZIP, extract the full folder, and run `LuigisMansionRecomp.exe`.
You can also update through F1 > Game > Updates. Update both players to this version.

No retail game data is included. Each player supplies their own USA Luigi's Mansion
disc dump (GLME01 revision 0). Keep existing saves backed up before experimental co-op.

Verification: native and distributable Windows builds; two local host/join instances;
Mario and Luigi skinning matrices compared against their own joint/bind-pose data;
host and join rendering inspected; packaged host at 120 FPS and client at 30 FPS checked
after movement/aim input; matching GameCube baseline preserved. These checks
do not establish a completed co-op campaign or separate-PC network reliability.

Based on rob646464/luigis-mansion-recompiled, with Aurora by encounter and the
SomeJakeGuy/lm-decomp reference. Existing notices and component licenses remain included.
