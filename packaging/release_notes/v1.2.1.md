**LmCoop 1.2.1 makes walking feel like a keyboard and mouse game.**

- **W A S D move relative to where you face.** Once you have moved the mouse, W goes the way Luigi
  faces, S backs away, A and D sidestep, and he keeps facing the mouse's way while he walks. Before,
  the game turned him toward his walking direction and fought the mouse.
- The arrow keys (C-stick) still turn him, and so does a gamepad's right stick.
- Scripted moments (hits, doors, cutscenes) are left to the game, and the facing picks up again
  from wherever the game left him.
- Needs the keyboard and mouse profile on port 1; with a gamepad on the port nothing changes.

Includes everything in 1.2.0 (joining the host game directly behind the loading screen) and 1.1.x:
instant mouse turning and up/down, no idle animation while you use the mouse, the visual controller
settings page and the start-screen version and update controls. No retail game data is included;
supply your own USA disc dump (GLME01 revision 0).

Based on rob646464/luigis-mansion-recompiled, with Aurora by encounter and the
SomeJakeGuy/lm-decomp reference. Existing notices and component licenses are included.
