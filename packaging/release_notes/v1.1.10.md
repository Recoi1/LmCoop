**LmCoop 1.1.10 adds native mouse turning and a visual controller settings page.**

- A GameCube-style controller diagram sits between the Control stick, D-pad, triggers,
  face-button and C-stick bindings.
- Player 1-4 tabs, input-device selection and binding profiles appear across the top.
- Click a binding to remap it; right-click to clear it. Primary and alternate bindings,
  conflict warnings, capture countdowns and saved profiles use the existing input system.
- Calibration, rumble, the full binding list, keyboard/mouse options, DSU and online
  controls remain available. The supplied logo was removed as requested.
- The same controller page is available from start-screen Settings and the F1 menu.
- **Native mouse turning:** moving the mouse left/right now turns Luigi (or Mario) 1:1 to where
  the mouse says, with no dead zone or snap-back. Before, it only nudged a virtual C-stick while
  the mouse moved. The game's own turning animation is kept. Mouse sensitivity still applies.

Includes the start-screen version/update controls and Mario skinning fix from previous
releases. No retail game data is included; supply your own USA disc dump (GLME01 revision 0).

Based on rob646464/luigis-mansion-recompiled, with Aurora by encounter and the
SomeJakeGuy/lm-decomp reference. Existing notices and component licenses are included.
