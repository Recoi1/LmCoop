## New in 1.1.6

- **Player names:** choose a name on the Multiplayer screen (up to 15 characters). Your partner's name shows above their head in game.
- **Mario is always player 2 online:** the guest now plays as Mario. Before, the guest was a second Luigi.
- Network protocol version 2: both players need 1.1.6. Older versions are refused with "version".

## Verification and limits

Checked with two copies on one PC: each side received the other's name, and the host showed the guest as Mario with the name above his cap. Online co-op remains experimental: ghosts, doors, cutscenes and map changes are not shared yet, and play between two real PCs over Radmin VPN has not been verified. Back up saves before trying multiplayer. This release contains a Windows package only.
