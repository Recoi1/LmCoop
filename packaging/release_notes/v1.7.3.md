**LmCoop 1.7.3: Luigi no longer slides into the wall when an online game starts.**

- **Fixed:** at the start of a multiplayer game (and during any scene that the game plays on its own) Luigi was pushed
  sideways every frame and slid across the foyer until he hit the wall by the stairs, where both players then stood
  stuck together. Cause: while a scene plays, player 2 is set aside and was pinned to exactly player 1's position on
  every frame. It is now only moved when it was left behind, and then to a spot beside player 1.
- Checked with a host and a guest on one PC (positions logged on both): Luigi stops by the carpet, as in a
  single-player start, and both PCs agree where everyone is. Before the fix he ended 925 units to the right.
- **Not fixed yet:** reports that the second player's controller does not work, and a door that opens at the start.
  Both are still being looked into.
- New test aids for bug reports: `LM_ONLINE_TRACE=1` and `LM_POS_TRACE=1` log where the players are;
  `LM_ONLINE_NOEVENTS=1` turns off the shared cutscenes.

Includes everything in 1.7.2 and earlier. No retail game data is included; supply your own USA disc dump
(GLME01 revision 0).

Based on rob646464/luigis-mansion-recompiled, with Aurora by encounter and the
SomeJakeGuy/lm-decomp reference. libdatachannel (MPL-2.0), MbedTLS and nlohmann/json are used, and the
Steamworks SDK's `steam_api64.dll` is redistributed with the program; see NOTICE.md.
