**LmCoop 1.1.11 makes mouse turning instant.**

- **Instant mouse turning:** moving the mouse left/right turns Luigi (or Mario online) at once, 1:1,
  with no lag. 1.1.10 steered the game's slow, easing C-stick, so he trailed behind the hand and
  drifted back. Now the mouse sets his facing through the game's own set-rotation routine, so the
  model, the flashlight direction and the game's turn state all follow in the same frame.
- **No idle animation while you use the mouse:** mouse movement now counts as activity, so Luigi's
  idle (AFK) animation no longer starts and turns him by itself while you play with the mouse.
- While you walk, the game still turns Luigi toward the way he is walking, which pulls against the
  mouse. Turning while standing or aiming is instant.

Includes everything in 1.1.10: the visual controller settings page, the start-screen version and
update controls and the Mario skinning fix. No retail game data is included; supply your own USA disc
dump (GLME01 revision 0).
